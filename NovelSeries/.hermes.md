## Editing Rules (Standing)

### Prose Style
- **Close third-person, intimate POV** — inside the character's head. Short, choppy sentences for internal states and emotional moments (not action).
- **Present tense for self-reassurance** — "It is fine" not "It was fine" when a character is repeating something to themselves in the present moment.
- **Three-part cadence** in prose when rhythm calls for it.
- **Replace periods with commas** to join closely related clauses for smoother rhythm ("Something changed there, something subtle" not "Something changed there. Something subtle.")
- **Remove em-dashes** — replace with commas, periods, ellipsis, or colons as the sentence structure requires. Not forced; use judgment.
- **Parallel grammatical structure** in emotional/physical sensation lists.
- **Natural language over clinical terms** — "chest" not "sternum." The prose is visceral.

### Repetition Rules (Critical)
- **Never repeat a character's name more than once per paragraph** — use pronouns or descriptors after the first mention. This is the #1 error caught by the user.
- **No three-part identical subject-verb repetition** — once a pattern is established, switch to pronouns or restructure.
- **Internal monologue progression** — when a character repeats a thought, escalate or shift, don't copy-paste. "It is fine." → "You are fine." → "Everything is fine."
- **Vary physical descriptions** across chapters — show the same character from a new angle, don't restage the same beat with identical wording.
- **Vary "She told herself..." structures** across chapters — don't use the same framing pattern twice.

### Line Editing
- Apply user's line-specific corrections IMMEDIATELY. No questions, no alternatives unless the edit would break the file.
- After every patch, READ THE PATCHED SECTION BACK to verify no duplication occurred.
- When patching files with markdown tables, verify table integrity after — the patch tool can corrupt `|` characters.
- When `write_file` refuses with `stale_write_blocked`, read remaining pages then retry or use `patch`.
- Use tight `old_string` spans — reading 5-10 lines of context before patching to avoid deleting adjacent narrative.

### Logic & Consistency Checks
- **Character world knowledge bleed** — never let a character think/use terminology they don't know. A human doesn't know about "mates" or "packs" until told in-story. A shifter doesn't react to something they've always known.
- **Cross-chapter scene overlap** — each chapter advances forward in time or shifts perspective. Never replay what the previous chapter covered.
- **Scene transition continuity** — the first 3-5 lines of a new chapter must connect to the last 3-5 lines of the previous chapter. No mid-action jumps without context.
- **Scene-state consistency** — WHO is WHERE must match between chapters. Read last 5-10 lines of prior chapter, compare to new chapter opening.
- **Cross-chapter prose descriptions** — scan previous chapters for physical descriptions that might repeat. Change at least two elements (posture, sensory detail, narrative angle).
- **Before writing, scan prior chapters** for established facts. Don't re-establish what was already shown.

## Amelia Wren (Book 1 Protagonist) — Specific Rules

- **HUMAN — NOT a shifter.** She does NOT know about shifters, mate bonds, or packs until Josiah explicitly introduces them in Chapter 9.
- **Cat: Luna** — female, scarred, slate-black, orange eyes.
- **Grandmother's packet scent:** fresh pancakes, bacon, and fried eggs.
- **Moon tattoo on left wrist.**
- **Healer/herbalist** living near shifter territory.
- **Grandmother's lessons:** phrase once as "Her grandmother taught her..." then switch to "She taught her..." to avoid repetition.
- After Ch 9, Amelia can use shifter terminology (Mate Bond, pack, Veil) in her internal narration naturally.

## Heat Level & Intimacy

- **Explicit/leaning explicit.** Write intimate scenes OUT — no fading to black, no implying, no skipping.
- Include sensory detail, emotion, and the bond's presence in intimate moments.
- **The bond is the primary vehicle for intimacy** — shared feelings, emotional vulnerability, not just physical touch.
- **Both characters must have agency** — Amelia is a researcher, healer, problem-solver. Victor struggles with control vs. connection. Relationship is a negotiation, not a fairy tale.
- **Mate bond distributed throughout the book** — physical sensations, emotional resonance, power sharing, vulnerability in every major act. Not just at the end.
- **Actual bonding beyond kissing** — domestic moments, waking together, quiet routines, sensing each other's emotions across distance.
- **Pre-ceremony 3-pace rule** — characters may share kissing, passion, romantic closeness, limited thread sensation, but must remain at least 3 physical paces apart before the Moonlit Rite ceremony. First full intercourse happens at the ceremony completion.

## Victor Slade — Specific Rules

- **Earned vs. worthy distinction** — Victor earned the Alpha position through blood and sacrifice, but does NOT feel worthy. He compares himself to his father Cyrus. Every time he walks past Cyrus's empty second-floor quarters, he sees the empty bed, the empty desk.
- **Cyrus's death** — killed by the Hollow King three years ago defending the northern boundary. Victor was too far away to help. This guilt defines Victor's self-worth.
- **Victor's voice** — Direct. Sparse. Short sentences when serious. Rarely jokes unless defusing tension. Internal monologue is analytical — always assessing threats, calculating risks.
- **Always use "Cyrus"** — never "old Alpha" or "predecessor" or "the previous Alpha." Names carry emotional weight.

## Pitfalls to Always Check

- **Dialogue attribution in alternating back-and-forth** — every non-obvious speaker transition needs a tag or action beat.
- **Pre-introduction character references in internal monologue** — scan for proper-noun references belonging to characters the POV character hasn't met.
- **Patch tool corrupting markdown tables** — verify after patching files with tables.
- **Patch appending instead of replacing** — especially with short targets and long replacements.
- **Patch span too broad, deleting adjacent narrative** — use tight matches.
- **write_file stale_write_blocked after partial read** — read all pages then retry, or use `patch`.
- **Character reactions to worldbuilding must reflect their knowledge level** — Josiah has centuries of memory; Perseus knows the Veil exists; David dismisses it. Don't have someone ask "What is the Veil?" if they should already know.
- **Never name the Veil or Hollow King in Chapter 1** — even in beat sheets or pack dialogue, describe as atmosphere.
- **Don't assume Alpha's first shift is combat** — he shifts all the time. Make it the first time a non-shifter (Amelia) FEELS it through the bond.
- **Don't let the bond solve everything** — it creates vulnerability too. The Hollow King targets it because it connects people.
- **Don't concentrate intimacy at the end** — readers expect emotional and physical connection distributed throughout.
- **After patching, verify actual file on disk** — the user's editor may cache stale content. Confirm on-disk state before declaring done.
