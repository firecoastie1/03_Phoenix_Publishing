# Chapter 37 — The Dawn

AMELIA

She wakes to quiet.

The room is pale with morning light. Dust hangs in the air. The compound is silent, not the dead silence from before, not the hollow silence of the Hollow King, but the exhausted silence of survivors.

Victor is beside her. Asleep. His breathing is slow. Uneven. His face is pressed into her shoulder. His arm is wrapped around her waist. His hand is curled against her ribs. He looks smaller in sleep. The silver light is gone from his skin. His eyes are gray. Tired. Real. Alive.

She does not move. She does not want to break the moment.

The bond is still humming between them. Warm. Strong. The barrier held. The Hollow King is gone. She can feel it, not through her senses, but through the bond itself. The cold presence that pressed against the wards has receded. The wards are intact. The pack is safe.

But she knows this is not the end.

She closes her eyes. Breathes. Feels Victor's heartbeat against her back. Steady. Slow. Alive.

The compound begins to stir. Voices in the corridors. Footsteps. Whispers. Pack members checking on each other. Assessing damage. Tending wounds. The morning after a battle.

She stays still until she cannot.

Victor shifts. Mumbles. His arm tightens around her. His face presses deeper into her shoulder. For a second, she thinks he will fall back asleep.

Then he opens his eyes.

"Morning," he says. His voice is rough. Tired.

"Morning," she says.

He does not move. His arm stays around her waist. His breathing stays slow. He is not getting up. Not yet.

"Victor?"

He closes his eyes. Nods once. "I am here."

"Victor, you need to — " She touches his arm. "Josiah is going to want to know what happened last night. The pack is going to want to know what happened. You need to — "

He opens his eyes. Looks at her. Gray eyes steady. Tired. But steady. "I will go when I am ready."

She nods. Does not push. He just channeled a Mate Bond through his own body, became a living shield, and held a barrier against the Hollow King's full assault. He is exhausted. She lets him rest.

But the compound is waking up. Voices are getting louder. Someone is shouting. Not in panic. In urgency.

Victor sits up. Immediately. The exhaustion is gone. Replaced by the Alpha. His jaw is set. His eyes are focused. He looks at her. "Stay here."

"Victor — "

"Stay here." He kisses her forehead. Once. Quick. "I will be back."

He gets up. Dressed in seconds. Leaves the room.

She lies in bed. Listens. The voices in the corridors are getting louder. Voices she does not recognize. Pack members she has not met. Some are shouting. Some are crying. Some are ordering.

She gets up. Dresses quickly. Pulls her hair back. Walks to the door.

Stops.

She can hear Victor's voice over the others. Calm. Firm. In command.

But underneath it, something else. Something she recognizes through the bond. Worry. Fear. Concern. Not for himself. For someone else.

She opens the door.

---

VICTOR

He walks into the corridor. The compound is a wreck. The wards held, but the impact left its mark, shattered glass, cracked walls, scorch marks on the floor. Pack members are everywhere. Some are tending wounds. Others are checking damage. Some are standing in groups, faces pale, whispering.

Victor steps into the chaos. His Alpha presence cuts through the noise. Voices drop. Eyes turn toward him. He walks through the compound like a general surveying a battlefield, assessing, calculating, making decisions.

Perseus is in the healing tent. Surrounded by wounded. Seven dead. He saw the battle through the bond. He knows what happened. His face is grim. He nods at Victor. No words needed.

David is pacing. Angry. Relieved. Terrified. All at once. "The wards held. I felt it. I felt the barrier and it — " He stops. Shakes his head. "You did it. You actually did it."

"I told you," Victor says. Calm. Steady. "We do this together."

David looks at him. Really looks at him. Victor's eyes are silver for a moment, just a flash, a shadow of the light from last night, and then they fade back to gray. But David saw it. He felt it. The barrier was real. The bond was real. And Victor almost died for it.

"Did you?" David asks quietly. "Almost die? I felt the bond. I felt it go wrong. For a second, I thought — " He stops. Shakes his head. "Did you almost die?"

Victor does not answer. Not yet.

Emila approaches. She is tending to wounds, bandaging, cleaning, stitching. Her hands are steady. Her face is calm. But her eyes are tired. She sees Victor. Looks at him. Really looks at him. "You are alive," she says. Simple. Direct.

"I am alive," he says.

She nods. "Good. Then come help. We have wounded. A lot of them. And one — " She stops. Swallows. Looks away. "One is hollowed."

Victor goes still. "Where?"

"East wing. Third room. He went down during the assault. We thought he was dead. But he is breathing. Heart is beating. Eyes are empty."

Victor closes his eyes. The hollowed warrior. One is hollowed. This is what Josiah warned about. This is what the Hollow King does, he does not kill. He empties. He leaves bodies breathing and minds gone. Puppets.

"Who is it?"

Emila hesitates. "Marco. Marco Hayes. He is — " She stops. Swallows. "He was a good warrior. Your friend. From before."

Victor's chest tightens. Marco. He remembers Marco. From the old days. Before Victor became Alpha. Before the war. A warrior who fought beside him. Laughed with him. Trusted him.

"Show me," he says.

---

He walks to the east wing. The corridor is quiet. Too quiet. The voices from earlier have faded. The wounded have been moved to the healing tent. The east wing is reserved for the dead.

Or the hollowed.

He opens the door.

Marco is lying on a cot. His eyes are open. Empty. His chest rises and falls. Steady. Mechanical. His hands are limp at his sides. His face is peaceful. Wrongly peaceful.

Victor approaches. Slowly. Carefully. He kneels beside the cot. Looks at Marco's face. The face of a friend. A warrior. A man who gave everything to protect the pack.

Now his mind is gone.

"I am here," Victor says softly. "I am here, Marco. I am not letting go."

He reaches out. Places his hand on Marco's shoulder. Feels the skin. Warm. Alive. But the bond — the bond is empty. No pulse. No presence. Just a shell. A vessel. The Hollow King took it all.

But Victor refuses. He refuses to put him down. Refuses to let the Hollow King win again.

He holds Marco's hand. Holds on. Pushes through the emptiness with everything he has — through the Mate Bond, the bridge he built with Amelia, the connection that held the barrier.

It should not work. Marco is hollowed. The Hollow King emptied his mind. But the Mate Bond is stronger. It bridges. It connects. It reaches.

And it answers.

The bond hums between them. Warm. Steady. Alive.

He closes his eyes. Breathes. Holds Marco's hand.

And knows — absolutely knows — that this is just the beginning. The Hollow King will return. He will keep coming. And every time, Victor will have to hold harder. Hold longer. Until he cannot hold anymore.

But he will hold.

For the pack. For the bond. For Marco.

For Amelia.

---

AMELIA

She waits outside the healing tent. The voices have died down. The compound is quiet. Morning light filters through the cracks in the walls. The barrier still hums, she can feel it through the bond, a steady, warm, unbroken presence. The Hollow King is gone. For now.

Victor comes out of the east wing. His face is tired. Pale. But his eyes are steady. He walks past her without stopping. Heads for the war room.

She follows.

"Victor."

He stops. Looks at her. "I am fine."

"You are not fine."

He does not argue. He never argues. He just walks. She walks beside him.

In the war room. Josiah is sitting at the head of the table. Staff in the floor. Eyes closed. Calm. The way he always is.

Victor sits across from him.

"Report," Josiah says.

"The barrier held. The Hollow King's beast form recoiled and fled. The wards are intact. The pack survived. Several are wounded. Seven dead." He pauses. Swallows. "One is hollowed."

Josiah's eyes open. He looks at Victor. Really looks at him. "You know what this means."

"I know."

"The Hollow King targets bonds because they are the only thing he cannot destroy from the outside. He cannot breach a barrier. He cannot break a bond that is strong. So he does the only thing he can, he hollows. He empties. He takes the people you love and turns them into puppets. He uses them against you. Against the pack."

"I know."

"Then you also know you cannot put him down."

Victor looks at Josiah. Gray eyes hard. "I will not."

Josiah nods. Once. "Then we will find a way to bring him back. If the bond is strong enough to hold a barrier, it may be strong enough to reach into the hollow. It is worth trying. It is the only option."

Victor closes his eyes. Nods. Once.

Josiah stands. Picks up his staff. "Rest. The pack needs you. And when you are rested, we will begin."

He leaves.

Victor sits in silence. The war room is quiet. The barrier hums. The bond hums.

Amelia stands beside him.

"Come on," she says. Soft. Gentle. "Let's go home."

He looks at her. Gray eyes tired. But alive.

"Yes," he says. "Home."

They walk out of the war room. Together. The barrier holds. The pack is safe. The Hollow King is gone, for now.

But Victor knows this is just the beginning.

And he will be ready.
