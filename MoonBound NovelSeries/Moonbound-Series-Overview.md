# Moonbound — Series Overview

## The Hook

In a world where the Moon Goddess has been waging a secret war for millennia, shifters exist in hiding — wolves, panthers, bears, foxes, eagles, dragons — each type scattered and isolated. The ancient enemy they fear is waking up. And her only weapon left is something she never planned to use: the Mate Bond.

When a traditional wolf Alpha is bound to a human woman — a healer who doesn't know shifters exist — she becomes the first link in a chain of bonds that could either save the shifter world or destroy it.

## Book 1: *The Willing Sacrifice*

**The Premise:** Victor Slade is the Alpha of the Blackwood wolf pack — disciplined, protective, and carrying the weight of his father's legacy on his shoulders. Amelia Wren is a human herbalist living quietly on the edge of shifter territory. They should never have met. But when the Moon Goddess Aurela binds them together through a Mate Bond, everything changes.

Neither understands what's happening. The bond begins as a physical pull — tingling hands, a weight in the chest, a sense of something vast and ancient recognizing something it has been waiting centuries to find. Victor doesn't want a mate. He doesn't want anything that takes control away from him. Amelia doesn't want a shifter. She wants a quiet life, and she doesn't understand why her hands won't stop tingling.

But then the hollowed appear.

A pack warrior is found stripped of everything — mind, memories, emotion. Still breathing. Still standing. Not himself. The Hollow King, an ancient demon prince who has been starving in the shadows for centuries, is waking up. And he has one strategy: divide the shifters. Isolate them. Feed on their fear. The Mate Bonds are Aurela's answer — but the Hollow King knows it too, and he is coming for Victor's bond first.

Book 1 is about a man who has spent his entire life believing that control equals safety, learning that the strongest thing he can do is let go. And a woman who has spent her life feeling like she doesn't belong anywhere, discovering that her humanity is not a weakness — it is the one thing the ancient darkness cannot consume.

## The Expanding World

Book 1 establishes the world. The subsequent books expand it:

- **Book 2 — *Shifted Trust*:** Perseus (the Beta) and Lyra Thorne, a panther shifter in Louisiana. Territory war. Enemies-to-lovers. The bond bridges wolves and panthers.
- **Book 3 — *Bound by Borders*:** David (the Lieutenant) and Hazel Wilder, a bear shifter in the Rocky Mountains. Physical conflict. Honor vs. instinct.
- **Book 4 — *The Skeptical Vixen*:** Grayden (youngest warrior) and Willow Dove, a fox shifter in the desert badlands. Trust vs. deception.
- **Book 5 — *Sins of Fire and Fur*:** Raine Blackwell (female warrior) and Kael Ashwood, a dragon shifter in volcanic territory. Power, control, and ancient grief.
- **Book 6 — *Stolen Currents*:** Samuel (the tracker) and Sable Roche, an eagle shifter in the northern peaks. Intelligence, redemption, and betrayal.
- **Book 7 — *Unleashed Hearts*:** The finale. All bonds unite. The Hollow King launches his final assault. Aurela reveals the full truth. Victor and Amelia stand at the center of a coalition they built one choice at a time.

## The Core Themes

**Tradition vs. Change:** Victor's entire identity is built on preserving what his father built. The series asks: what does it mean to protect something by refusing to let it change?

**Choice Over Fate:** The Mate Bond recognizes, but it does not force. Every step of every relationship is a choice. The series is deeply committed to this — fated mates don't mean forced romance. The romance is earned, the trust is built, the love is chosen.

**Humanity as Strength:** Amelia is not a shifter. She cannot shift, cannot fight, cannot sense the supernatural. But her humanity — her capacity for empathy, for trust, for seeing people rather than labels — is what makes her essential. By the finale, it is her humanity that is the Hollow King's weakness.

**Connection as Power:** The Hollow King feeds on isolation. He thrives on pack politics, distrust, and division. The series' thesis is simple: connection is a weapon. Not magic, not destiny — connection. People choosing to stand together, even when it's terrifying.

## What Kind of Book Is This?

- **Fated mates** who must earn their love, not skip to it
- **Pack politics** with real consequences — hierarchy, tradition, dissent
- **A shared supernatural threat** (the Hollow King) that escalates across all seven books
- **Multi-shifter world-building** — wolves, panthers, bears, foxes, eagles, dragons — each with distinct cultures, abilities, and territories
- **Explicit romance** — the spice is real, character-driven, and advances both the relationship and the plot
- **Dark but not gratuitous** — morally gray characters, some violence, but no abuse. Protective alphas who respect their mates' autonomy
- **Found family** — the pack as home. Amelia, as a human, is the ultimate test of what "family" means

## The Tone

Imagine sitting around a fire with people who love you — the warmth, the intensity, the way someone can hurt you without meaning to and the way you forgive them anyway. It's intimate and loud, dangerous and safe, funny and devastating in the same breath. The series balances dark supernatural warfare with genuine humor and found-family warmth. Think Suzanne Wright's humor and multi-shifter world-building layered with Jessica Hall's dark intensity and Kristin Coley's pack dynamics.

## Why This Series?

Most shifter series keep mates within the same species. Moonbound crosses species lines organically, building a coalition one bond at a time. The Hollow King is not a rival pack or a rogue shifter — he's an ancient predator that feeds on shifter magic, and his strategy is psychological as much as physical. Amelia is not a liability waiting to learn to shift — her humanity is her strength, and it is the key to defeating the enemy. And Aurela, the Moon Goddess, is not purely benevolent — she is calculating, desperate, and willing to use mate bonds as strategic tools. The magic has cost. The choices have consequences.

## The Ending (No Spoilers, Just Scope)

Book 1 ends with the hollowed warrior found — proof the Hollow King is real, he is hunting, and the Mate Bond is his first target. Book 7 ends with all shifter types united, the Veil either collapsing or being remade, and Amelia's humanity — always the key — becoming the one thing the Hollow King cannot understand, cannot consume, cannot survive.

The series is about people learning that the thing that scares them most — connection, vulnerability, letting go of control — is also the thing that saves them.
