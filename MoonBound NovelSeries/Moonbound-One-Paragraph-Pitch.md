# Moonbound — One-Paragraph Pitch

**Moonbound** is a multi-shifter paranormal romance series about a traditional wolf Alpha who is bound to a human healer by an ancient Mate Bond — and the centuries-old war against a demon that feeds on isolation. When the Hollow King wakes and begins hollowing shifters, stripping them of mind and soul, the Moon Goddess's only weapon left is a coalition of bonds between different shifter types and humans. Book 1 follows Victor and Amelia as they learn that the Mate Bond doesn't create love — it creates a bridge, and they have to choose to walk across it together while an ancient darkness tries to destroy what they're building. Seven books. Wolves, panthers, bears, foxes, eagles, dragons. Fated mates. Earned love. Connection as a weapon.
